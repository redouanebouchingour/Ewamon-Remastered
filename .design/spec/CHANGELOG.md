## 2026-10-21 · `00-spine.md` §3.15 Nemeses (new), §4.4 (`nemesisAmbush`, `nemesisNews`), §4.7 (`GET /nemeses`, history `nemesis`), §5.2 (`NemesisRepo`, migration 0017); `30-server-netcode.md` §30.1.2 (`NEMESES`), §30.4.3a (nemeses); `20-client.md` §20.9.9p (new); `50-verification-budget.md` §50.4.2 / .4 / .5 / .7 rows · nemeses
Why: the user approved a nemesis system (like Shadow of Mordor's): a wild creature that knocks out your lead or escapes your ball gets a name, a scar, levels and a new move, remembers you, ambushes you in other zones with a taunt about the last fight, and becomes a trophy when beaten or keeps its name and scar when caught; darkened zones breed more of them. The calls: per player, world wild battles only (never a legendary, the roamer, a static, a trainer or ghost, the tower, the arena or a raid); the trigger is a knockout or an orb broken out of, with the battle ending with it neither caught nor knocked out (run, white out); promotion chance 0.6 to 1 and ambush chance 0.08 to 0.16 by the zone's shadow level; at most 3 active, the oldest fades; +2 to +5 levels, rank ups +1 to +2 (rank 1 to 5); the gained move is the next level-up move above its level, else a damaging TM; the scar is a torn ear for an orb, else the mark of the hardest blow it took (by move type); an ambush is held between lead - 2 and lead + 3 so a dark zone means more nemeses, never harder ones; a repel does not stop one; the trophy goes to the creature that dealt the last damage; a caught nemesis keeps its name as nickname and its scar as a ribbon (`nemesis-scar:<scar>`); in a Nuzlocke / Hardcore run an ambush is that zone's encounter like any wild battle. Server env `NEMESES` (on, off in testEnv, `always` for live checks); no core change.
Evidence: `packages/content/test/nemesis.test.ts` (6) and `pnpm --filter @ewa/content validate`; `packages/persist/test/nemeses.test.ts` (2) and `migrations.test.ts`; `packages/protocol/test/rest.test.ts` (100 routes) and `server-messages.test.ts` (27 types); `packages/server/test/nemeses.test.ts` (5); `packages/client/test/nemesis.test.tsx` (9); singles and doubles fuzz digests unchanged; live on the first isolated pair with `NEMESES=always` (a Brookrawl broke out of a Spark Orb on Tide Route and rose as Murkjaw the Drowned; the Profile's Nemeses list showed it; on Mire Route it ambushed at Lv 23 against a Lv 25 lead, its lower third saying "Murkjaw remembers your Spark Orb.
