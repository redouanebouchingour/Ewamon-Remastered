Image 1 is the new front sprite of Acier, a Steel/Fighting creature from our monster-collecting game. Image 2 is Acier's old back sprite. Image 3 is a reference sheet of its four swords, left to right: A, B, C, D.

Draw the BACK battle sprite of the creature in image 1: the same creature seen from behind, in a 3/4 rear view facing away toward the upper right, framed and posed like image 2 (wide stance, seen from behind and slightly to the side).

Correct anatomy, very important: exactly FOUR arms and no others, two shoulders on each side of its broad back, an upper and a lower shoulder. Each arm is one solid, continuous limb joined to the torso, no gaps, no floating parts, no arm crossing over the head. Each fist grips exactly one sword:

The four swords, the same as in image 1 and image 3, simplified into pixel art:

- C, Asta's Demon-Slayer Sword (Black Clover): a huge, thick, broad black blade with pale grey-white crackle patches, a gothic-arch point, a black crossguard with flared ends, a round pommel.
- D, Guts' Dragon Slayer (Berserk): a very long, straight, wide slab of grey iron with a groove down the middle, a sharp point, a chunky block crossguard, a brown leather grip.
All four swords fully visible, none hidden behind the body, not touching the canvas edges.

From behind show: the grey riveted plates of the back and the four shoulders, the back of the dark helm with the edge of the gold visor-mask just visible at the side, the gold belt, the backs of the gold greaves, the white-tipped feet. Same colours as image 1.

Art style: match image 1 and image 2 exactly, the same big chunky square pixels (crisp and hard-edged), the same thick dark outline, flat 2 to 3 tone shading. No gradients, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no fine texture. Full body, centered. Do not draw an aura, glow, flames or smoke.
Background: one perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF, no gradient, no ground shadow. No magenta or hot pink in the creature or the swords. No text, no letters, no frame, no second creature. Square 1:1.
