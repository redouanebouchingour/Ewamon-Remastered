Thick dark outline around the whole silhouette. Flat cel shading with only 2 or 3 tones per colour, hard edges between tones. Big simple shapes, a clean readable silhouette, large expressive eyes. No fine fur or feather texture, no gradients, no soft airbrush shading, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no painterly detail.
