Image 1 is a battle sprite from an indie creature-collecting RPG called Ewamon. Draw ONE new original creature in
exactly the same pixel art style as image 1, but do not copy its design.

Pixel art style (most important): a true low-resolution pixel art sprite drawn on a grid of about 100 by 100 pixels,
then enlarged with nearest-neighbour scaling so every art pixel is a big, crisp square block, exactly like image 1.
Thick dark outline around the whole silhouette. Flat cel shading with only 2 or 3 tones per colour, hard edges between
tones. Big simple shapes, a clean readable silhouette, large expressive eyes. No fine texture, no gradients,
no soft airbrush shading, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no painterly detail.

Creature: Kelvyre, a mythical Fire and Ice creature made in a ruined laboratory by fusing two elemental cores into one
body. A tall, lean, upright humanoid feline with a smooth rounded head, two short swept-back horns, narrow glowing eyes,
a long thick tail, strong digitigrade legs and long arms with three blunt fingers on each hand. Its body is split
exactly down the middle from the top of the head to between the feet:
- its LEFT half (the side nearer the viewer's left) is living FIRE: dark charcoal-red skin with glowing molten orange
  cracks, a crest of flames rising from the horn and shoulder on that side, an ember-orange eye, flickering flame tufts
  on the elbow;
- its RIGHT half is glacial ICE: pale icy blue crystal skin with white frost facets, sharp ice spikes on the horn,
  shoulder and forearm on that side, an ice-blue eye.
A thin bright white-gold seam glows along the centre line where the two halves meet. Around its neck sits a broken
metal containment ring, melted and dripping on the fire side, frosted over on the ice side. Its long tail curls out to
the side: icy blue crystal along its length, ending in a burning orange flame at the tip. One hand is raised at chest
height, a small flame floating over the fire palm. Calm, cold, superior expression.

Pose and framing: front battle sprite, 3/4 view facing slightly to the left, full body, standing, arms held close to
the body, centered, with empty space around it, the same scale as image 1.

Colours: fire half #3a1a1a, #c2361c, #ff7a2a, #ffc24a; ice half #2f6fa8, #7cc3ee, #d8f2ff; seam #fff3c4; dark outline
#2a2d3c.

Background: perfectly flat solid magenta #ff00ff, the same as image 1, no shadow, no scenery. No magenta or pink on
the creature. No text, no border, no watermark, only one creature.
