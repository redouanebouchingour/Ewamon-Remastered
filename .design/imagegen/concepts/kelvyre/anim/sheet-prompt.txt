Image 1 is an animation sheet for a pixel art battle sprite from an indie creature-collecting RPG called Ewamon. It
holds 8 copies of the same creature, Kelvyre, in a 4 by 2 grid on a flat magenta background. Turn it into the 8
frames of one smooth, looping idle animation, read left to right, top row then bottom row; frame 8 must flow back
into frame 1.

Keep everything that is not listed below EXACTLY as it is in image 1: the same design, the same pixel grid (every
art pixel is a crisp 3 by 3 block), the same colours, the same outline, the same size and the same position of the
creature in its cell. The feet never move. This is an edit of the existing sprite, not a redraw: do not change the
head shape, the horns, the face, the broken metal ring around the neck, the ice spikes, the lava cracks or the
proportions.

What moves, and only this:
- Every flame (the flame floating over the raised hand, the flame on the elbow, the flame crest on the head and the
  flame at the tail tip) flickers: its tongues change shape a little in every frame, rising and curling upward,
  growing slightly taller in frames 2 to 4 and smaller in frames 6 to 8, like a real fire loop.
- A gentle breath: in frames 3 to 5 the head, shoulders, arms and chest sit 1 art pixel (3 screen pixels) higher;
  the legs and feet stay still.
- The tail sways slowly: its tip moves up by 1 to 2 art pixels in frames 3 to 5 and back down.
- In two or three frames a tiny white frost sparkle (a 1 or 2 pixel plus sign) appears next to an ice spike.

Pixel art rules: hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no motion blur, only the colours already
in the sprite (flame tongues use its orange, yellow and pale yellow). Background stays perfectly flat magenta
#ff00ff in every cell, no shadow, no grid lines, no text, no numbers, no border.
