Image 1 is an animation sheet for a pixel art battle sprite from an indie creature-collecting RPG called Ewamon. It
holds 8 copies of the same creature, Kelvyre, in a 4 by 2 grid on a flat magenta background. Turn it into 8 frames of
a looping fire animation, read left to right, top row then bottom row; frame 8 must flow back into frame 1.

Keep the creature's body EXACTLY as it is in image 1 in every frame: the same design, the same pose, the same pixel
grid (every art pixel is a crisp 3 by 3 block), the same colours, the same outline, the same size and the same
position in its cell. Do not move or redraw the head, horns, face, neck ring, arms, legs, ice spikes, lava cracks or
tail. Only the fire changes.

The fire: the flame floating over the raised hand, the flame on the elbow, the flame crest on the head and the flame
at the tail tip burn strongly and lively. In every frame each flame has a clearly different shape: tongues split,
curl and rise, a small ember (one or two pixels) sometimes breaks off the top and floats up. Frames 1 to 4 the flames
lean and lick to the left and grow up to about one third taller than in image 1; frames 5 to 8 they lean to the right
and sink back to their normal size. The flame over the hand is the biggest and most active.

In frames 3 and 7 a tiny white frost sparkle (a 1 or 2 pixel plus sign) appears just outside one of the ice spikes.

Pixel art rules: hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no motion blur, flames only in the orange,
yellow and pale yellow already in the sprite, with the same dark outline. Background stays perfectly flat magenta
#ff00ff in every cell, no shadow, no grid lines, no text, no numbers, no border.
